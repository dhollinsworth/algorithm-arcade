Try the switches: 1 means ON, 0 means OFF. The total is shown in denary.

Denary (base 10) is our everyday number system. Binary (base 2) uses only 0 and 1. Each binary digit is a bit. From right to left, the column values double: 1, 2, 4, 8… A 1 means include that value; a 0 means leave it out.

1101₂ = 8 + 4 + 1 = 13₁₀

To make 13 in binary, switch on 8, 4 and 1. Keep 2 off. Leading zeros do not change the value: 00001101 also means 13. All numbers here are unsigned (zero or positive).
